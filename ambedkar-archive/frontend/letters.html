1927-04-11 ARCHIVAL
To: Bhaurao Gaikwad
From: Dr. B.R. Ambedkar
Damodar Hall, Parel, Bombay. I am sending here Gangavane to make collection on behalf of our society at this meeting on the occasion of this yatra. The association is a bogus body & besides our people must be told...

From: Dr. B.R. Ambedkar
My dear Bhaurao, This is to introduce to you my friend Mr Chitre. He will tell you the purpose for which he is seeing you. Please do what he will tell you. He has my support and you will therefore not hesitate...

1928-11-25 ARCHIVAL
To: Bhaurao Gaikwad
From: Dr. B.R. Ambedkar
Damodar Hall, Parel. Dear Bhaurao, I cannot accept 30th for his appeal. I will let you know in a few days what date will suit me. It might be better if you can tell me when the Devale people will be ready...
